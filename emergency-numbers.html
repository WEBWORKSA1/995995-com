What to say

How to make an emergency call

1. Location first

Street address, landmark, or GPS coordinates from your phone's map app. If the call drops, they can still find you.

2. What happened

"A man has collapsed and isn't breathing." Number of people hurt and any dangers (fire, traffic, weapons).

3. Don't hang up

Answer questions and follow instructions. The dispatcher may coach you through CPR until help arrives.

Can't talk?

Many countries offer text-to-emergency (e.g. text 911 in parts of the US and Canada, SMS 70995 in Singapore, 999 BSL/SMS in the UK after registration). Check locally.

Travelling

Save the destination's ambulance number in your contacts as "ICE Local" and add your own ICE (In Case of Emergency) contact.

Set up Emergency SOS and Medical ID on your smartphone — responders can see allergies and contacts from the lock screen.
