Privacy policy

Last updated: September 2026

Summary

You can use 995995 without an account. We collect only what you choose to send us through forms, plus limited technical data needed to run and fund the site. We never sell your personal information.

Information you give us

When you submit a form (contact, quote request, donation pledge, contest entry, job application, newsletter), we receive the details you enter. Forms are delivered to our private inbox through a third-party form relay (FormSubmit). For quote requests you explicitly consent to, we share your request with up to three matched providers.

Stored on your device

Your country choice, theme, accessibility settings, checklist progress and family-plan entries are saved in your browser's local storage only. They never leave your device and you can clear them at any time.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet. You may opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. If you choose "Essential only" in our cookie banner, we request non-personalised ads. See how Google uses data from partner sites.

YouTube videos

Videos use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode and load only when you press play. After that, YouTube's own privacy policy applies.

Hosting & logs

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log IP addresses for security. Fonts are served by Google Fonts.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, PIPEDA, DPDP Act and others), you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. Contact us and we'll respond within 30 days.

Children

995995 is a general-audience site. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the age required in your country) without parental consent.

Changes
