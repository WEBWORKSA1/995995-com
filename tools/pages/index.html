The 4 C's

In any emergency, do this first

1

Check

Is the scene safe for you? Don't become a second casualty — look for traffic, fire, electricity, water.

2

Call

Call the local emergency number (or have someone else call). Say where you are first, then what happened.

3

Care

Give first aid you're able to: CPR if not breathing normally, firm pressure for heavy bleeding.

4

Comfort

Stay with the person, keep them warm and reassured, and follow the dispatcher's instructions.

FAQ

Common questions

Does 112 work everywhere?

112 works across the European Union and on most GSM mobile phones in many other countries, where it is redirected to the local service. It is not universal — always check the country's own number in our directory.

Can I call an emergency number without credit or a SIM?

In many countries mobile phones can reach emergency numbers without credit, and some networks allow calls without a SIM. Rules vary by country — never rely on it; keep a charged phone with a working plan.

Is 995995 an official emergency service?

No. 995995 is an independent information website. We do not dispatch help and are not affiliated with any government or emergency service. In an emergency, call your official local number.

How accurate is the information?

Numbers are compiled from public sources and reviewed regularly, and first-aid steps follow published guidance from international resuscitation councils and Red Cross / St John first-aid manuals. Numbers can change — report a correction and we'll fix it fast.
