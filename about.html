💡 Our mission

Nobody should lose a life because they didn't know the number.

995995 exists to put the right emergency number and the right first steps in front of anyone, anywhere, in seconds.

Why "995995"?

Short, repeatable numbers are easy to remember under stress. Around the world, three-digit numbers like 112, 911, 999 — and 995 in some places — are the fastest route to help. Say it twice and you get our name: a promise to help you twice as fast.

The name is used purely descriptively. 995995 is not affiliated with any emergency service, government agency or organisation that uses those digits.

What we believe

  • Free for everyone. No paywalls, no sign-up walls.
  • Fast beats fancy. Every page is lightweight and works on slow connections.
  • Accuracy is non-negotiable. We cite sources, date our reviews and fix errors quickly.
  • Accessible to all. Larger text, readable fonts, dark mode, keyboard navigation.

How we stay accurate

Numbers are compiled from national emergency-service sources and public listings and re-checked on a schedule. First-aid steps follow published guidance from international resuscitation councils and Red Cross / St John first-aid manuals. Reader corrections are prioritised.
